# Elenta Office — live floor, full org (3 levels)

The live interactive floor wired to the real 115-person grouped org
(generated from ../../../orgs/elenta.json into org-live.json):

- **Office**: Command (Boss, 2) · Military group (8 departments, 108) · Business (5).
- **Military group**: the 8 departments (Intelligence, Operations & Plans,
  Information & Civil-Mil, Cyber EW & Space, Warfare Doctrine, Logistics &
  Engineering, Capability & Technical, Personnel Med & Legal) with per-department
  status, clickable.
- **Department**: that department's sub-teams and desks; click a worker for a
  status card. Status rings, sidebar counts and the department list all derive
  from the same people data.

Pan/zoom, rotate, Fit, light/dark. Sample agent state — the next step is wiring
the status/counts to Buzz (observer/typing signals) so it shows actual teammates.

Published artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/47rrEsphiwhefsKytGWJSH
Assets are the dev pack's illustrations (PNG workstations, SVG screens/markers).
