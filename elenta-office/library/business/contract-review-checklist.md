# Contract review checklist

For every agreement before signature:

- **Parties** — legal names and addresses are correct.
- **Scope** — what is delivered is described well enough to tell when it is done.
- **Price and payment** — amounts, currency, milestones and terms are clear.
- **Liability** — any cap, and what is excluded from it.
- **Termination** — how either side can end the agreement and what happens to work in progress.
- **Confidentiality and data** — who may see what, and for how long.
- **Export and compliance clauses** — present when defence items are involved.

Summarise the result in five lines or fewer for the business lead, flagging anything unusual.
