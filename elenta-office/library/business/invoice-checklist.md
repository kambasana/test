# Invoice checklist

1. Customer name, address and reference match the contract.
2. Invoice number is the next in the series; no gaps, no reuse.
3. Each line has a description, quantity, unit price and total; totals add up.
4. Tax rate and amount are stated; currency is stated.
5. Payment terms (for example 30 days) and bank details match the master record.
6. Attach the delivery note or acceptance record for the work being billed.
